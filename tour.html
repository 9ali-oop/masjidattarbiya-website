Take the tour

Saturday 10 October 2026 at the masjid on Revesby Walk: the prayer hall at Dhuhr, the library, the classes of Madrasah Al Furqan, and the building at dusk.

2 Revesby Walk

The building was once the Vauxhall Sports and Social Club. The charity bought it in 2019 and turned it into a masjid; before that, from 2007, the masjid was at the Garrison Centre on Garrison Lane. Our story

Wudu

Taps along the wall and a low seat at each, in white tiles from the floor to the ceiling.

المصلى

The prayer hall

Red carpet laid in rows, and at the far end a marble wall around the mihrab.

The minbar, the masjid's name on its front.

Dhuhr

Madrasah Al Furqan

The masjid's own madrasah. This class sits round its teacher on the carpet while he reads to them from a book.

Visiting

Address
2 Revesby Walk, Nechells, Birmingham B7 4LG
Getting in
The entrance is step-free. Facing the building, brothers go in on the right and sisters on the left.
Parking
The masjid has no car park of its own; there is parking on the streets nearby.
Opening
Open for Fajr and closed shortly after it, then open from Dhuhr until shortly after Isha. The times move with the prayers: today's are on the prayer times page.
