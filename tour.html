الشارع

Revesby Walk

2 Revesby Walk, Nechells, Birmingham B7 4LG. In 2019 the charity took on the building, the former Vauxhall Sports and Social Club, and set about turning it into a masjid; the story is on Our Story.

The masjid has no car park of its own; there is parking on the streets nearby.

The entrances

Facing the masjid, brothers go in on the right and sisters on the left, by their own entrance to the sisters' prayer area. The entrance is step-free.

The prayer hall

The five daily prayers are prayed here in congregation, and Jumu'ah every Friday. The khutbah is usually in Arabic and English, sometimes in English only. Today's times are on the prayer times page.

مدرسة الفرقان

Madrasah Al Furqan

The masjid's own madrasah teaches here six days a week, with no classes on Friday. Children learn the Qaidah, then the Qur'an, and tarbiyah. The groups and times are on the madrasah's page.

الغروب

At dusk

The masjid opens for Fajr and closes shortly after it, then opens again from Dhuhr until shortly after Isha. The times move with the prayers.

Before you come

Address
2 Revesby Walk, Nechells, Birmingham B7 4LG
Getting in
The entrance is step-free. Facing the building, brothers go in on the right and sisters on the left.
Parking
The masjid has no car park of its own; there is parking on the streets nearby.
Opening
Open for Fajr and closed shortly after it, then open from Dhuhr until shortly after Isha. The times move with the prayers: today's are on the prayer times page.
Jumu'ah
One Jumu'ah every Friday. The khutbah is usually in Arabic and English, sometimes in English only.
