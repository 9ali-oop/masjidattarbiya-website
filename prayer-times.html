Reading the timetable

Begins
The start of the prayer's time.
Iqamah
When the congregation stands to pray in the masjid. Aim to arrive a few minutes before.
Jumu'ah
One Jumu'ah each Friday, in place of Dhuhr. The khutbah is usually in Arabic and English, sometimes English only. The khutbah time and the iqamah are shown separately.
Sisters
There is a prayer area for sisters, with its own entrance on the left of the building as you face it. Brothers enter on the right.
Where the times come from
The masjid publishes its timetable on Masjidbox. This site copies it every night, and says so plainly if it ever cannot, rather than showing old times.
