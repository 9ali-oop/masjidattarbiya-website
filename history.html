From a pub to a masjid

The journey of this building, and the community that changed it.

A building on Revesby Walk that once served alcohol now calls people to prayer five times a day. That is a story worth telling properly, which means telling it accurately.

This page is unfinished on purpose. The dates we can evidence are here. The ones we cannot are marked as open questions rather than guessed at, because a masjid's history is worth more than a good-sounding version of it. If you know an answer, we would like to hear from you.

Help us fill in the gaps

If you prayed here in the early days, helped with the building work, or have photographs from before it looked like it does now, please get in touch. Old photos on a phone are exactly what this page needs.

We are especially looking for the name of the pub, the year the community took on the building, and the date of the first prayer held here.
