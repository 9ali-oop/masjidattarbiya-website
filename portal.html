How it works

Three steps

  1. Register your child

    Send us their name, age and school, by WhatsApp today or through the online form when it opens. One message per child is fine.

  2. We confirm a place

    The madrasah replies with a place in the right group, 6 and over or 12 and over, or adds your child to the waiting list if the group is full.

  3. Log in to the parent app

    Once your child is enrolled you get a login: attendance, messages from the teachers and fees, on the web or in the app for iPhone and Android.

Questions parents ask

Before you register

Who can join?

Children aged 6 and over. There are two groups: 6 and over, and 12 and over. Your child is placed by age and by how far along they are in reading the Qur'an.

When are the classes?

Six days a week, with no classes on Friday. Saturday runs for most of the day. The exact times are on the madrasah page; they are marked To be confirmed until the madrasah confirms them, so please check with us before relying on them.

How much does it cost?

Fees are To be confirmed. Ask on WhatsApp and we will tell you the fee, and how to pay, before you commit to anything.

What will you ask me for?
  • your name, phone number and an emergency contact
  • your child's name, date of birth, school and Qur'an reading level
  • anything we need to know to keep your child safe, if there is anything

Nothing else. You do not need any documents to register.

What is the parent portal?

A website and app the madrasah uses to take the register, message parents and record fees. It is run for the madrasah by Teach 'n Go, a school management service used by other madrasahs in Birmingham. Parents get a login once a child is enrolled, and the app works on iPhone and Android.

When does the portal open?

It is being set up now. Until it opens, WhatsApp and phone do the same job: the madrasah registers your child and answers your questions. Parents will be told by WhatsApp, and at the madrasah, when the login is ready.

I don't use WhatsApp

Call 07908 854187, email info@masjidatarbiya.org, or ask at the masjid after any prayer.

Who can see my family's details?

Only you and the madrasah's staff. Teach 'n Go stores them for the madrasah under UK and EU data protection law and does not use them for anything else. The full privacy notice explains what is kept and for how long.

Have these ready

  • your child's full name and date of birth
  • the school they go to
  • how far they have got with the Qur'an: starting out, reading the qaidah, or reading the Qur'an
  • a second phone number for emergencies

Registering takes two minutes by WhatsApp. If anything here does not work for you, the masjid can register your child in person after any prayer.
