What we ask for, and why

Only what the madrasah needs to teach your child and keep them safe:

  • your name, phone number and an emergency contact
  • your child's name, date of birth, school and Qur'an reading level
  • anything we need to know to keep them safe, if there is anything

The portal is run for the madrasah by Teach 'n Go, a school management service based in Ireland, under UK and EU data protection law. Only you and the madrasah's staff can see your family's details. Read the full privacy notice.

Need help?

If anything here does not work for you, the masjid can register your child by phone or WhatsApp instead.
