What we ask for, and why

Only what the madrasah needs to teach your child and keep them safe:

  • your name, phone number and an emergency contact
  • your child's name, date of birth, school year and Qur'an reading level
  • anything we need to know to keep them safe, if there is anything
  • whether you agree to photographs

Only you and the madrasah's staff can see your family's details. They are never sold or shared. Read the full privacy notice.

Need help?
