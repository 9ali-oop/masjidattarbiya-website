Our Story

Attarbiya Masjid

Attarbiya Masjid is located at 2 Revesby Walk, Nechells, Birmingham B7 4LG. We are a registered UK charity (No. 1142204), serving as a place of worship and a community centre for local Muslims.

Our charity's roots go back to 2006, first established to serve Birmingham's Somali community under the name Somali Education and Cultural Centre, and formally registered with the Charity Commission in 2011. In 2021, we moved into our current home on Revesby Walk - a former sports and social club building that we converted into the masjid and community centre you see today.

Our Mission

What We Stand For

Worship

We host all five daily congregational prayers and Jumu'ah, so the community can pray together throughout the day.

Education

Our Qur'an and Islamic studies classes serve children and adults alike, building knowledge and connection to the deen at every age.

Community

Beyond the prayer hall, the masjid is a gathering point for the local community throughout the year.
