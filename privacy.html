Privacy

What this website does and does not do with your information.

The short version

The masjid's pages do not ask you for any information, and we do not track you. There are no analytics and no advertising, and we set no cookies of our own. The one exception is the Madrasah Al Furqan parent portal, which exists so that parents can register a child with the madrasah, and which is explained in full below.

What happens when you visit

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, their servers receive your IP address in order to send you the page, and they may keep short-term logs for security and abuse prevention. We have no access to those logs and we do not receive any visitor statistics.

What is loaded from elsewhere

Being straight about this, because these are the only ways your details reach anyone else without you clicking:

  • The map on the Contact page. That page embeds a Google map, so Google receives your IP address and may set its own cookies. No other page loads it.
  • The parent portal, once it opens. Registering a child or logging in takes you to Teach 'n Go, the service the madrasah uses to manage its classes, described below. Our own portal page loads nothing from them until you choose to go there.

Everything else comes from this website. Our typefaces are served from here rather than from Google, and prayer times, events and talks are plain files on this site, so reading them involves nobody but us.

If you donate

Donations are handled entirely by GoCardless, a payments provider authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority. When you choose an amount you leave this website for theirs.

Your bank details never touch this website. We cannot see them and we do not store them. GoCardless collects your name, email address and bank details in order to set up the payment, and their privacy notice governs that. The masjid receives a record of the donation and the donor's name so that we can account for the money properly, as a registered charity must.

The Madrasah Al Furqan parent portal

The portal is not open yet. Before it opens, the trustees will confirm the details in this section, including exactly who at the madrasah can see registrations and how long they are kept. Until then, nothing is collected through it, and parents register by WhatsApp or phone.

When it opens, this is how it will work:

  • Who runs it. The madrasah uses Teach 'n Go, a school management service run by Teach 'n Go Ireland Limited in Dublin. They hold the information on the madrasah's behalf, under a data processing agreement, and are bound by UK and EU data protection law. The charity remains responsible for it.
  • What we ask for about you. Your name, a phone number, an email address and an emergency contact.
  • What we ask for about your child. Their name, date of birth, school, Qur'an reading level, and anything we need to know to keep them safe (such as allergies or medical needs). Health information is only collected if you choose to give it, and only used to keep your child safe.
  • Why. To run the madrasah: to place your child in the right group, to keep a register, to contact you, to manage fees, and to look after your child while they are with us.
  • Who can see it. You can see your own family's details in the parent portal and app. The madrasah's teachers and the staff named by the trustees can see what they need to teach and look after your child. It is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • Payments. If you pay fees online through the portal, the card payment is handled by the payment provider Teach 'n Go uses. Your card details never reach the madrasah.

If you contact us

There is no contact form on this site. If you call, message us on WhatsApp or send an email, we keep what you send only for as long as we need to deal with it, and we do not add you to any list.

Links to other places

Where we link to Masjidbox, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Google Maps or the Charity Commission, those are other people's websites with their own privacy notices. Following a link is your choice.

Photographs

We only publish photographs of identifiable people where we have their permission, and where a child is identifiable we ask a parent or guardian first. If a photograph of you or your child appears here and you would like it removed, contact us and we will take it down.

Your rights, and how to reach us

Under UK data protection law you can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
