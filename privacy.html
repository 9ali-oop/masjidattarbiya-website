Privacy

What this website does and does not do with your information.

The short version

This website does not ask you for any information, and we do not track you. There are no forms, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. We set no cookies of our own.

What happens when you visit

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, their servers receive your IP address in order to send you the page, and they may keep short-term logs for security and abuse prevention. We have no access to those logs and we do not receive any visitor statistics.

The one thing loaded from elsewhere

Being straight about this, because it is the only way your details reach anyone else without you clicking:

  • The map on the Contact page. That page embeds a Google map, so Google receives your IP address and may set its own cookies. It is the only embedded thing on the whole site, and no other page loads it.

Everything else comes from this website. Our typefaces are served from here rather than from Google, and prayer times are a plain file on this site, so reading them involves nobody but us.

If you donate

Donations are handled entirely by GoCardless, a payments provider authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority. When you choose an amount you leave this website for theirs.

Your bank details never touch this website. We cannot see them and we do not store them. GoCardless collects your name, email address and bank details in order to set up the payment, and their privacy notice governs that. The masjid receives a record of the donation and the donor's name so that we can account for the money properly, as a registered charity must.

If you contact us

There is no contact form on this site. If you call, message us on WhatsApp or send an email, we keep what you send only for as long as we need to deal with it, and we do not add you to any list.

Links to other places

Where we link to Masjidbox, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Google Maps or the Charity Commission, those are other people's websites with their own privacy notices. Following a link is your choice.

Photographs

We only publish photographs of identifiable people where we have their permission, and where a child is identifiable we ask a parent or guardian first. If a photograph of you or your child appears here and you would like it removed, contact us and we will take it down.

Your rights, and how to reach us

Under UK data protection law you can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
