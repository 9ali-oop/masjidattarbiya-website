About the masjid

Attarbiya Masjid is a registered charity at 2 Revesby Walk in Nechells. The five daily prayers are held in congregation, with one Jumu'ah every Friday, and the building is also home to Madrasah Al Furqan, the masjid's own madrasah for children aged 6 and over.

The charity was founded in 2006, and the masjid first opened in 2007 at the Garrison Centre on Garrison Lane. It has been on Revesby Walk since 2019, when it took on the former Vauxhall Sports and Social Club and set about turning it into a masjid. For adults there are evening lessons, day classes and winter conferences, many of them recorded, and Al Kissaii, the masjid's youth programme, runs youth nights for teenagers.

Visiting

Address
2 Revesby Walk, Nechells, Birmingham B7 4LG
Entrances
Facing the masjid, brothers enter on the right and sisters on the left. There is a prayer area for sisters.
Opening
The masjid opens for Fajr and closes shortly after it, then is open from Dhuhr until shortly after Isha. The times move with the prayers: see prayer times.
Access
The entrance is step-free. The masjid has no car park of its own; there is parking on the streets nearby.
Jumu'ah

One Jumu'ah every Friday. The khutbah is usually in Arabic and English, sometimes in English only.

For this Friday's time, see prayer times.
