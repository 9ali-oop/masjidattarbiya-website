About Us

A place of worship, learning, and community

Attarbiya Masjid and Kowneyn Community Centre is at 2 Revesby Walk, Nechells, Birmingham. We are a registered UK charity, number 1142204, serving the local Muslim community with daily congregational prayers, Jumu'ah, and Qur'an and Islamic studies classes.

صلاة

Salah

The five daily prayers in congregation, and Jumu'ah every Friday. The times on this site come straight from the masjid's own timetable.

Knowledge

Madrasah Al Furqan for children, and for adults evening lessons, tafsir, day classes and winter conferences, with talks recorded for YouTube.

Community
