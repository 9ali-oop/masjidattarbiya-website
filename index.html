About Us

A place of worship, learning, and community

Attarbiya Masjid and Kowneyn Community Centre is at 2 Revesby Walk, Nechells, Birmingham. We are a registered UK charity, number 1142204, serving the local Muslim community with daily congregational prayers, Jumu'ah, and Qur'an and Islamic studies classes.
