Madrasah Al Furqan

Part of Attarbiya Masjid

Under one roof

The madrasah is part of the masjid

Al Furqan is not a separate organisation. It is the teaching arm of Attarbiya Masjid, in the same building and run by the same charity. Its name is one of the names of the Qur'an: al-Furqan, the Criterion, which tells truth from falsehood. It is also the title of the twenty-fifth surah.

The same building

Classes are at the masjid, 2 Revesby Walk, Nechells, Birmingham B7 4LG.

The same charity

Run by Kowneyn Education and Cultural Centre, registered charity 1142204, whose registered activities are an after-school club and supplementary education.

Safeguarding

The charity's policies on file with the Charity Commission include safeguarding, complaints, and bullying and harassment.

Registering your child

How it works

Go to the parent portal
  1. Fill in the registration form

    One short online form for each child: their name, date of birth, school and Qur'an reading level, and anything we need to know to keep them safe.

  2. The madrasah confirms a place

    We will be in touch to offer a place in the right group, or to add your child to the waiting list if a group is full.

  3. Use the parent app

    Once your child is enrolled, you get a login for the parent portal and app: attendance, messages from the teachers, and fees, all in one place.
