Islamic Education

Building strong foundations in Qur'an and deen

Our Madrasah offers structured Qur'an memorisation (Hifdh), Tajweed, and Islamic Studies classes for children and adults, taught in a supportive and disciplined environment. Whether your child is just starting to learn their letters or is progressing through the Qur'an, our teachers work with each student at their own pace.

  • Qur'an & Tajweed

    Correct recitation and pronunciation, from beginners through to advanced reciters.

  • Hifdh (Memorisation)

    Structured memorisation with regular revision, for students aiming to complete or progress through the Qur'an.

  • Islamic Studies

    Aqeedah, Seerah, and fiqh essentials taught in an age-appropriate way for children and adults.

  • All Ages Welcome

    Classes run for children, teenagers, and adults — it's never too late to start learning.
