# Madrasah Registration — Google Form Setup

Go to forms.google.com → Blank form. Title it "Masjid Attarbiya Madrasah Registration". Add these questions in order:

1. **Student's Full Name** — Short answer, required
2. **Student's Date of Birth** — Date, required
3. **Parent / Guardian Full Name** — Short answer, required
4. **Parent / Guardian Phone Number** — Short answer, required
5. **Parent / Guardian Email** — Short answer (validate as email), required
6. **Home Address** — Paragraph, optional
7. **Which programme(s) are you registering for?** — Checkboxes, required
   - Qur'an Program
   - Islamic Studies
   - Arabic Language
   - Quran Intensive Course (Summer)
   - Not sure / need advice
8. **Preferred class days/times** — Checkboxes or short answer (match to whatever schedule you confirm for madrasah.html)
9. **Has the student attended Madrasah classes before?** — Multiple choice: Yes / No
10. **Any medical conditions, allergies, or additional needs we should know about?** — Paragraph, optional
11. **How did you hear about us?** — Short answer or dropdown, optional
12. **Additional comments** — Paragraph, optional

Once built:
1. Click **Send** (top right).
2. Choose the **`< >`** embed tab.
3. Copy the URL inside `src="..."` from the code shown.
4. Send me that link — I'll drop it into `registration.html` in place of the placeholder iframe, and the page goes live.

Optional: under Settings (gear icon) → Responses, turn on "Collect email addresses" and "Send responders a copy" so parents get a confirmation.
