Safeguarding

Keeping children and adults safe at the masjid and in Madrasah Al Furqan, and what to do if you are worried about someone.

If someone is in danger now

Call 999. Once they are safe, tell us what happened.

If you are worried about a child

Tell us: speak to the child's teacher or the madrasah's management, or call or WhatsApp the masjid on 07929 242914. We will listen, write down what you tell us and pass it on to the people who can help.

You do not have to come to us first. You can always go straight to Birmingham Children's Trust, which handles concerns about children in Birmingham:

  • Birmingham Children's Trust: 0121 303 1888, in office hours.
  • Out of hours: 0121 675 4806.
  • For advice, if you are not sure: the NSPCC helpline, 0808 800 5000.

If you are a child or young person

If something is wrong, tell a teacher you trust. If you would rather talk to someone outside the masjid, call Childline on 0800 1111. It is free, and the number does not show up on most phone bills.

If you are worried about an adult

If an adult who needs care and support is being abused or neglected, or is at risk of it, tell us, or contact Birmingham City Council's adult social care team:

  • Adult social care: 0121 303 1234, in office hours, or report a concern online.
  • Out of hours, if it is urgent: 0121 464 9001.

If your worry is about someone who works or volunteers here

That includes a teacher, an imam, a volunteer or a trustee. You do not have to tell us first: contact Birmingham Children's Trust on 0121 303 1888, or the police. Concerns about adults who work with children are looked into by Birmingham's Local Authority Designated Officer (LADO), independently of us, on 0121 675 1669.

Our safeguarding policy

The trustees are finalising a safeguarding policy for children and adults, written for Birmingham and for the madrasah's classes. It covers how concerns are recorded and passed on, safer recruitment including DBS checks, and how children are taught and supervised. It will be published on this page, with the names of the people who lead on safeguarding, once the trustees have adopted it.
